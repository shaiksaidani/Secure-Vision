What is checked

  • Exact match with a protected original (SHA-256)
  • Exact match with a personalised copy issued to someone
  • Invisible watermark in images, and who it was issued to
  • Visual similarity to protected images (modified copies)
  • Access history, devices and related security events

Watermarks survive copying and cropping, but not JPEG re-compression, resizing or screenshots. Results never claim more than the evidence shows.
